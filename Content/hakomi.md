## Hakomi is a gentle and powerful approach to transformation.

Hakomi is a body-centered psychotherapy method that facilitates change through experience.

Mindfulness serves as a foundation for the work to study how suffering manifests in our whole being. With this approach of curious and compassionate study, we allow vulnerable parts to surface, be held, and heal.
