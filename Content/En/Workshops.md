## What is it?

These practices come from the international mindfulness community. They are well established in California, Germany and Scandinavia but still rare in France. They offer a kind of **relational mindfulness**: exploring together, safely, what we experience in the present moment when we are with others.

You might hear something like: "Being here with you, I notice an urge to speak… and some embarrassment. Now that I'm speaking, my hands are getting sweaty, but seeing you listening, I relax a little." Someone may then share how that lands for them, or ask to know more.
:::

::: steps
## The 5 principles of Circling

1. **Commit to connection**
2. **Stay with sensation**
3. **Own your experience**
4. **Trust your experience**
5. **Be with the other in their world**
:::

::: facts
## What to expect

- **Small group** · Evenings of about 2.5 hours
- **A clear frame** · Consent, the right to pass, confidentiality, no alcohol or substances
- **No prior experience** · Guided exercises in pairs and groups
- **Not therapy** · These are relational group practices, not group therapy
:::
