# Frequently asked questions
:::

::: faq
### What is a "psychopraticien"?
It is the French professional title for a practitioner offering psychological and body-centred support who is neither a medical doctor nor a psychologist. A **psychologist** holds a university master's degree in psychology. A **psychiatrist** is a medical doctor who can diagnose and prescribe. The title psychopraticien is not state-regulated, which is why I am transparent about my training (see [About](/en/about/)) and work in regular supervision.

### How is this different from talk therapy?
We do talk. But we also pay attention to what happens in your body while you speak: a tension, a movement, a feeling coming up. These signals often give more direct access to what hurts and to what can change.

### Does it work online?
Yes. Most of the work is about attending to your inner experience, which translates very well to video. You just need a quiet, private space, a stable connection and, ideally, a camera that shows your upper body.

### I don't feel much in my body. Is this for me?
Yes, that's very common. We start where you are, at your pace. Noticing little is already useful information.

### Do you work with couples?
Yes. Couple work rests on the same principles: slowing down, noticing what happens in each of you and between you, and opening new ways of relating. Let's talk about it during the discovery call.

### How many sessions will I need?
It depends on what brings you. Some people come for a few sessions around a specific question; others for longer work. We check in regularly, and you can stop at any time.

### Which rate should I choose?
Choose freely, without justification, between €40, €65 and €89 per 1-hour session, according to your means. See [Sessions & fees](/en/sessions-and-fees/).

### Is it covered by insurance?
Usually not, since I am not a licensed health professional. Please check with your insurer. I can provide an invoice.

### What happens in the discovery call?
A free 20-minute video call. You tell me what brings you, I answer your questions, and we see whether we'd like to work together. No commitment.

### Is what I say confidential?
Yes. In supervision, cases are discussed anonymously.

### What if I'm in crisis?
This work is not suited to emergencies. Call 112 or your local emergency number, or a local crisis line. In France, 3114 (suicide prevention) is free and available 24/7.
