## Hi there, glad you're here

My name is Olivier. I lived abroad for 15 years, and I offer therapy in English in person in Strasbourg. My work is informed by the Hakomi method, a mindfulness-based therapy method that focuses on the connection between the mind and body. I often work with people wanting to explore relational patterns, but the method is suitable for a wide range of situations and challenges.

I have hundreds of hours of training in Somatic Therapy in English with the Hakomi Institute of California and a Bachelor's degree in Psychology from the University of Strasbourg.

I have experience working in English and with international people. My method is experiential, inspired by psychodynamic approaches, attachment theory, polyvagal theory, and trauma-informed therapies.
