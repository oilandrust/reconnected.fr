## Data protection

**Data controller:** Olivier Rouiller, o.rouiller@gmail.com.

**Data collected:**

- when you email me: your email address and the content of your message;
- when you use the contact form: name, email address and the content of your message;
- when you book an appointment: name, email address, chosen time slot, the information you enter in the booking form and, where applicable, payment data (processed directly by the payment provider: I have no access to your bank details);
- if you sign up for workshop updates: your email address.

**Purposes and legal bases:**

- answering your requests and organising appointments: pre-contractual measures and performance of a contract;
- invoicing: legal obligation;
- workshop updates: your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.

**Recipients and processors:** Cal.com, Inc. (booking), Formspree, Inc. (contact form), Stripe (payment), Google (email), GitHub (hosting). Some are based in the United States; transfers are covered by the European Commission's standard contractual clauses and/or the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

**Retention periods:**

- contact requests without follow-up: 1 year;
- appointment data: 3 years after the last contact;
- invoices: 10 years (accounting obligation);
- workshop list: until you unsubscribe.

**Your rights:** access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and portability, by writing to o.rouiller@gmail.com. You may lodge a complaint with the CNIL, the French data protection authority ([www.cnil.fr](https://www.cnil.fr)).

**Session data:** what you share in sessions is confidential. Any session notes are stored securely and never shared with third parties.

## Cookies

This site uses **no analytics or advertising cookies** and does not itself set any cookies requiring consent.

Page views are measured with **GoatCounter** (EU-hosted), without cookies or advertising tracking: only aggregated page-view statistics are collected. See [goatcounter.com](https://www.goatcounter.com/).

On the [Contact](/en/contact/) page, choosing an appointment type loads the **Cal.com** booking calendar (a third-party script). Cal.com may set its own cookies and, where required, ask for your consent, under its own cookie policy. The [Sessions & fees](/en/sessions-and-fees/) page embeds a **Google Maps** map, which may set its own cookies under Google's policy. The contact form sends your message to **Formspree** only when you submit it; no Formspree script or cookie is loaded on the site.
