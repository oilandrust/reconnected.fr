## Sessions

- **Length** · 1 hour
- **Format** · Online by video. In person in Strasbourg, France: coming soon
- **Languages** · English or French
- **Time zones** · Sessions are scheduled in your local time. I am based in Central European Time.
- **Who** · Adults, individually or as a couple
- **Rhythm** · Usually weekly or every two weeks. We decide together, and you can stop whenever you wish.

::: pricing
## Sliding scale

I want this work to stay accessible, so the same 1-hour session is offered at three rates. **Choose freely, no questions asked.**

### Supported
**€40** / session

Students, people between jobs or on a low income.

### Standard
**€65** / session

The rate that suits most people.

### Supporter
**€89** / session

If your income allows: it makes the supported rate possible for others.

---

As a guideline, clients living in higher-income countries (e.g. Western Europe, the UK, North America) usually choose the standard or supporter rate. If your situation changes, just let me know. For couple sessions, let's talk about it during the discovery call.
:::

::: cards
## Payment and cancellation

### Payment
Payment details are shared during the discovery call. Prices are in euros. An invoice is available on request.

### Cancellation
Sessions cancelled or moved less than 48 hours in advance are due, except in case of emergency.

### Insurance
I am not a licensed health professional, so sessions are usually not covered by health insurance. Please check with your insurer if unsure.

### Confidentiality
What you share in sessions stays confidential. I am in regular supervision, where cases are discussed anonymously.
