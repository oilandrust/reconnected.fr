## About Olivier

I have been passionate about mindfulness, embodiment, and transformation for close to 10 years, and I am training to become a psychologist and somatic therapist.

I was born in France and lived for 15 years around Europe, in Germany and Sweden. This experience provided me with a multicultural lens and let me appreciate how much culture shapes our behaviors, relational patterns, and wounds. I now live in Strasbourg, France, where I study Psychology at the University.

For 10 years, I worked as a Software Engineer programming Computer Games.

Besides transformation, I am passionate about movement, dance, somatic, and relational practices.
