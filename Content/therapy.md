# Somatic Attachment Therapy and Coaching

A thread in my personal growth has been to study and heal the way we are in our bodies in relationship with others.

I believe that at the intersection of Trauma, Attachment, Relational Work, and Embodiment lies a great potential for transformation. Through this work, we can reclaim our authentic selves in relationships.

At the core of my inquiry is a personal journey of understanding what is in the way of creating meaningful relationships.

This questioning led me to the study of attachment, complex trauma, shame, etc. This opened up the way for a holistic and integrative work with people from all horizons.
